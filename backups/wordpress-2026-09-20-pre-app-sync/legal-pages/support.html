Write to support@forgenexxus.com from the email on your account and we will pick it up. Include the name of the site and, if it is about a build, roughly when you sent the prompt.

Quick answers

I signed in on my phone. Why do I need to sign in again here?

Each device keeps its own session, the way chat apps do. Use the same email, Google or Apple account and everything you built is already here.

Where are my credits?

On the credits card in the sidebar and under Plan & credits. The number is the same in the app and on the web because it lives in one place.

A build failed. Was I charged?

No. A request that fails is released in full. Usage shows every movement, newest first.

How do I use my own domain?

Domains are part of the Premium plan. Add the hostname under Domains, point its DNS at the address shown, and publish; the domain stays pending until publishing verifies it.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. It removes your sites, domains, credits and history and cannot be undone.
