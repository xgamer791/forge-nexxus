This policy explains what Forge Nexxus collects when you use the mobile app or the website at forgenexxus.com, why, and what you can do about it. The two are one product on one account, so the same policy covers both.

What we collect

  • Account details. The email address, name and sign-in method you use (email link, Google or Apple). We never see a password because there is none.
  • What you build. The prompts you send, the pages Forge builds in reply, the names of your sites, their published addresses and any custom domains you point at them.
  • Plan and credits. Your plan, credit balance and the ledger of what each request cost. Card details go to Stripe, our payment processor, and never touch our servers.
  • Settings. Appearance preferences you choose, stored against your account so they follow you between devices.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs (IP address, browser, time of request) kept for security and reliability.

How we use it

To run the product: to build and edit your sites, to serve them at their addresses, to meter credits, to send you the sign-in email you asked for and to answer support. Your prompts and pages are sent to the AI provider that generates them, under our own account, in order to produce the result you asked for. We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising.

Where it lives

Your account and everything it owns are stored in our database provider, Convex, and are the same records whether you signed in on the mobile app or the web. Published sites are served from the same deployment. Sign-in emails are sent through Resend. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Your choices

  • Change your name and see your linked sign-in methods under Profile.
  • Unpublish or delete any site at any time.
  • Cancel a plan from Plan & credits; you keep your credits until the period ends.
  • Delete your account from Profile. This removes your sites, domains, credits, history and sign-in records. It cannot be undone.

Cookies and storage

We use browser storage only for your session tokens and a copy of settings the server already holds, so the app can paint before it hears back. There are no tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@forgenexxus.com. We will update this page when the policy changes and note the date here.
